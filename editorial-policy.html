Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Editorial Policy describes the standards that guide content on Tech With Aditya, an independent technology information website created by Aditya Prajapati.

Original Content

All articles published on the website are intended to be original. We do not publish plagiarized, copied or spun content. Content must provide genuine value to readers.

Research and Fact-Checking

Topics are researched using reliable sources where appropriate. For changing technology subjects, readers are encouraged to verify details through official documentation. Fabricated statistics, fake news and invented facts are not permitted.

Use of Reliable Sources

When external information is referenced, preference is given to primary and reputable sources. Links to sources may be included where helpful.

Corrections

If a factual error is identified, it will be corrected promptly. Significant corrections may be noted on the article page. Readers can report potential errors via the Contact page.

Updating Outdated Information

Technology evolves. Articles may be updated when practical to reflect major changes. The “Last updated” date is shown when an article has been revised.

Sponsored Content and Affiliates

If sponsored content or affiliate links are ever used, they will be clearly disclosed. At present, the site focuses on independent educational content. Any future commercial relationships will be labeled transparently.

AI-Assisted Content

AI tools may assist with research, outlining or drafting. Every published article is reviewed, edited and improved by the author (Aditya Prajapati). Automatically generated low-value content is not published. The goal is useful, human-reviewed material.

What We Do Not Publish

  • Plagiarized or spun articles
  • Fabricated facts or statistics
  • Fake reviews or testimonials
  • Misleading clickbait designed only to attract clicks
  • Pages created mainly for advertisements
  • Content that primarily manipulates search rankings without reader value

Tone and Clarity

Content is written in clear, accessible language suitable for beginners and intermediate readers. Technical terms are explained when used.

Contact

Questions about editorial standards can be sent via the Contact page.
