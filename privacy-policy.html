Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tech With Aditya (“the website”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit the site. The website is an independent technology information platform operated by Aditya Prajapati.

Information We Collect

At present, the website is primarily static and does not require user accounts. Information may be collected in the following limited ways:

  • Contact form: If you submit the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you provide are collected so we can respond. This information is used only to handle your inquiry.
  • Newsletter (if activated): If a newsletter signup is later connected to an email service, the email address you submit would be used solely for sending optional updates. You would be able to unsubscribe at any time.
  • Server / hosting logs: Standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps) may be generated by the hosting provider for security and technical operation.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website currently does not set non-essential cookies for advertising or tracking. If cookies or local storage are later used for essential functionality (for example, remembering a preference), this policy will be updated.

If third-party services such as analytics or advertising networks are added in the future, those services may set their own cookies. Details will be disclosed here before activation.

Analytics

No third-party analytics service is currently active on this website. If analytics (for example, privacy-friendly or Google Analytics) is added later, this section will be updated to describe what is collected and how it is used.

Advertising and Google AdSense

The website is prepared for possible future use of Google AdSense or similar advertising networks. At the time of writing, advertising is not active. If advertising is enabled:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Users may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through available tools such as Google’s Ads Settings or industry opt-out pages.
  • This policy will be updated with specific details once advertising is implemented.

We do not encourage users to click advertisements and do not design pages solely for the purpose of showing ads.

How Information May Be Used

  • To respond to contact form submissions
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website
  • To protect against abuse or security threats
  • To comply with legal obligations if required

We do not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. Server logs are typically retained according to the hosting provider’s policies. You may request deletion of information you have submitted by contacting us.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to submit the contact form.
  • You can use browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • If a newsletter is activated, you can unsubscribe via the link in any email.

Third-Party Links

Articles may contain links to external websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy policies of third-party sites.

Children

The website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the Contact page or email the address listed there once provided by the owner.
